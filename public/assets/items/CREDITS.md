# Item artwork credits

Artist: Henrique Lazarini (7Soul1).
Collection: **496 pixel art icons for medieval/fantasy RPG**, repackaged by gnola14.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/496-pixel-art-icons-for-medievalfantasy-rpg
Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/496_RPG_icons.zip
License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Retrieved: 2026-09-18.

This game bundles 41 unmodified 34×34 PNG files selected from that collection.
The source page explicitly labels this compilation CC0 and states that icons
incompatible with public-domain distribution were removed from this repack.
Credit is retained in appreciation of the artist, even though CC0 does not
require attribution. No runtime connection to the source site is needed.

The item-to-file mapping is maintained in `src/game/assets/itemArtwork.ts`.
Items not in the mapping retain the game's original procedural artwork.
Do not assume unrelated artwork on OpenGameArt or the artist's other packs
has the same license.
